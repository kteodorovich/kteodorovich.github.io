In my last semester at Purdue, I worked on a small research project under my Complex Analysis professor, Kiril Datchev. It used concepts from complex analysis and linear algebra, as well as pushing me to pick up some basics of quantum mechanics and scattering theory. The project examined how resonance multiplicity affects resolvent and wave expansions. More specifically, I worked on constructing an explicit resolvent expansion composed of generalized resonant states.

I am immensely grateful to have had this opportunity, though I regret that I never quite finished the project. Still, I learned a great deal that semester. Below are some of the key references I spent the most time with:
* [Introduction to Quantum Mechanics -- David J. Griffiths](https://www.fisica.net/mecanica-quantica/Griffiths%20-%20Introduction%20to%20quantum%20mechanics.pdf)
* [Mathematical Theory of Scattering Resonances -- Semyon Dyatlov, Maciej Zworski](https://math.mit.edu/~dyatlov/res/res_final.pdf)
* [SNAP 2019: Scattering Theory Lecture Notes -- Peter Hintz](https://sites.psu.edu/phintz/files/2025/07/snap.pdf)
